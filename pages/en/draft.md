# DRAFTOUT: exclusive-cell duels

This is BoardRaid’s private two-player non-overlapping ruleset. It retains the task competition and drafting core, but each cell may belong to only one player. A goal claimed by the opponent cannot score for you, adding contention and trade-offs to route planning.

## Starting a match

1. Create a private two-player room with a 5×5 board.
2. Select the non-overlapping duel mode, apply the rules and wait for both players.
3. Complete drafting and preparation according to the selected room rules; follow the in-game board.

## Ownership, victory and meta goals

The first player to claim 13 of the 25 cells wins, rather than the standard 15-goal or full-board target. Meta goals are excluded to avoid conflicts between exclusive ownership and goals based on completing other cells. Near-simultaneous claims are resolved by server-accepted ownership, not local notification timing.

## Limits and common misconceptions

This ruleset is limited to private duels and 5×5 boards, not solo, multiplayer or other sizes. It is not a synonym for drafting: ordinary drafting can produce goals both players may finish, whereas this mode enforces exclusive ownership during play. It does not automatically become a rated match.

## Planning your route

During drafting, consider your own route and goals the opponent may reach first. Prioritize contested objectives that fit your route and keep alternatives ready instead of investing in already claimed cells. Individual goal descriptions still define completion.
