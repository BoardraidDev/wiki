# Treasure and portal races

## Portal race
Players race in their own worlds to complete the portal objective. Wait for target loading and the synchronized countdown. The current match UI states the score target.
Stronghold replacement and draw requests use the votes shown in two-player play.
## Treasure hunt
The 2-column, 3-row board requires an axe, pickaxe, shovel, bucket, wooden door and wooden boat. Any accepted material variant counts. Complete all six tasks to win the round.
Moving to the next treasure starts fresh inventory, task progress and vanilla advancements for the round.
## Custom practice
Stable 0.5.5 supports these modes in solo and private two-player rooms. Unrelated main-game settings do not apply.
Solo practice has no two-player draw or forfeit vote; replacing the target acts immediately. Do not assume every multiplayer room size supports races.
