# Main game and drafting

## Board and win conditions
Complete goals on the board. Stable ranked play uses 15 completed goals as its win threshold. Custom line and full-board modes follow their own room settings.
Read each goal and required count. Obtaining an item and earning an advancement are not always the same condition.
## Draft phase
On your turn, choose from the offered goals. Watch the timer, round and player state. Some configurations generate a board directly.
## Preparing to start
Adjust settings before the countdown ends. Do not use previews or external state readers to gain information before play.
## Match actions
Seed changes and draw requests follow the votes shown by the room. Resetting your own world is a separate operation and does not reset the opponent.
## Results
Results show the outcome and recorded progress. Preserve logs when reporting an abnormal result instead of repeatedly reconnecting.
