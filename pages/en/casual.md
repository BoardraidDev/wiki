# Casual modes

## Entry and ranked differences
Select Casual on the home screen, then choose an activity. Casual matches do not change ranked points; custom rooms are a separate entry for your own rules. Activities depend on server capabilities and client compatibility. Right-click an activity button for its in-game rules.
## Classic 5×5
Play on a 5×5 task board, racing to complete 15 goals. It reuses main-game task tracking with casual-oriented difficulty. Choosing this queue does not expose all custom-room settings; the activity supplies its matchmaking configuration.
## Portal race
Race through stronghold objectives in the same order in separate worlds with the same seed. Each confirmed objective earns a point; first to 5 wins. There are at most 3 target-replacement requests per match and both duel players must agree. Wait for loading and countdown; visiting an arbitrary stronghold does not substitute for the current objective.
## Treasure hunt
Follow the same buried-treasure route and complete the 2-column, 3-row tool/item board: axe, pickaxe, shovel, bucket, wooden door and boat. Complete a round for one point; first to 5 wins. Inventory and progress reset for the next target. Route replacement requires duel agreement; see the race chapter and current in-game prompts for the full round requirements.
## Most%: timed progress
Available when enabled by the server. Compare main-game progress over 20 minutes in worlds with the same seed, using a random seed if the library is unavailable. A 10-second opening vote defaults to using unlimited elytra, but both players must select it. When enabled, death or a seed change replenishes elytra and infinite fireworks. An agreed seed change clears progress and restarts time. Equal progress is a draw, and independent world reset is not allowed.
## Queueing, cancellation and room exclusivity
Once queued, the corresponding button becomes Cancel matching. Closing the screen does not leave the queue: cancel explicitly. Do not join multiple queues; leave an existing custom room before matchmaking. Queue controls and in-match actions are separate.
## Requests, results and replay
Draw, seed and target changes follow the activity's voting rules; requesting is not approval. Independent world reset differs from seed replacement. Review score, tasks and match details after completion. Replay availability depends on actual uploaded records and retention, not a promise of permanent access.
