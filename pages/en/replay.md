# Replays and history

## Open a replay
Choose a recording from the client replay menu. History describes the recorded match, not a current room. A missing recording may not have uploaded or may have expired.
## Camera and timeline
Switch lanes and choose first person, third person or free camera. Click the timeline to seek. Left and right arrows seek five seconds; Space controls pause.
Holding Space in free camera still flies upward. Recorded health and hunger are shown only in first person when data exists.
## Seeking and dimensions
Large jumps and dimension changes require terrain and entity restoration. Report persistent black screens with match identifier, lane, timestamp and client version.
## World resets
Segments before and after resets should follow match time. Report repeated or misplaced segments without publishing account information.
